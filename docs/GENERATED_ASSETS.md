## `public/assets/celestial-school.jpg`

Legacy generated concept from the earlier design pass. The revised experience does not load it.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: marketplace landing-page hero image
Primary request: An original luminous vision of an old Brooklyn school transformed into an ornate celestial art sanctuary, floating high in pale blue clouds.
Scene/backdrop: Endless powder-blue sky, soft cloud banks below and behind the building, restrained tiny celestial glints.
Subject: A monumental symmetrical brick school with arched windows, balustrades, bell-tower-like details, and a grand stair, translated into a refined pixelated architectural diorama; the building should feel inhabited by art, with a few warm white windows.
Style/medium: High-end editorial 3D illustration with deliberate chunky pixel/voxel edges, subtly reminiscent of late-1990s rendered game architecture but materially luxurious; original, not based on any existing game or artwork.
Composition/framing: Panoramic landscape; building slightly right of center with usable quiet sky on the left for interface copy; low horizon of clouds; strong readable silhouette.
Lighting/mood: Heavenly morning light, icy white highlights, gentle atmospheric bloom, serene and rarefied rather than cute.
Color palette: Pale sky blue, icy white, washed cobalt shadows, very sparing warm pearl light.
Materials/textures: Glazed brick, frosted stone, translucent cloud vapor, crystalline window highlights.
Constraints: No text, logo, watermark, people, religious symbols, fantasy creatures, or marketplace UI.
Avoid: Purple gradients, gold excess, generic AI fantasy castle, photorealistic modern skyscrapers, dark night sky.

## `public/assets/physical-works.jpg`

Asset type: marketplace catalog artwork contact sheet
Primary request: A perfectly aligned 3-column by 2-row contact sheet containing six distinct, original physical artworks, photographed as installation/catalog images for a discerning contemporary-art marketplace.
Scene/backdrop: Each of the six equal panels is edge-to-edge and self-contained, with no gutters, borders, or labels; quiet pale gray-blue gallery walls.

Style/medium: High-end museum catalog photography, tactile and believable, each work materially distinct and physically constructible.
Composition/framing: Exact 3×2 grid of six equal panels, viewed straight on; artwork fills most of each panel; consistent gallery distance; every panel suitable for cropping independently by fixed thirds and halves.
Lighting/mood: Soft skylight, cool serene atmosphere, subtle grounded shadows.
Color palette: Pale blue, icy white, cobalt, charcoal; very restrained brick red and warm pearl.
Constraints: Precisely six panels; no people, text, logos, overlapping frames, or watermark.
Avoid: NFTs on screens, psychedelic gradients, fantasy imagery, mock UI, duplicate works, gilded frames.
